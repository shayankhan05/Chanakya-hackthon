What is proven

Mandatory tasks and dependencies are hard constraints. Selected tasks respect budget, work hours, declared staffing and finish windows. The exact discrete search maximizes user-entered advisory importance. Tie-breaking is deterministic; minimum cost and makespan are not additional objectives.

Scope and limits

Up to 12 tasks, 3 people, 14 days and 50,000 nodes. Whole-hour durations. Tasks keep one person across consecutive available working slots and pause outside working hours. Larger inputs are rejected; reaching the node limit means optimality or feasibility may be unknown. An LLM cannot override the solver.
