This guide explains how the current ComplianceCopilot app works, what its visible controls do, and how to
use them in everyday business situations. It describes the implemented application rather than the original
feature wish list. Repeated controls are grouped where their behavior is identical. Conditional controls are
identified so that you know why a button may be absent. All company stories, wages, preparation costs and dates in examples are illustrative. Your account uses your
own saved business information. The app helps you organize and review obligations; government registration,
filing, payment and legal confirmation happen outside this app. How to use this guide
Read Sections 1 to 3 for the workflow. Use the contents to find a screen, then look for its button tables. Sections 14 to 18 explain growth, financial planning, scheduling, AI and calculators. Section 21 gives
complete worked journeys.

1 What the app does
ComplianceCopilot connects your business information with legal screening, actions, dates, records and
growth planning. Instead of searching through separate spreadsheets, you can see why an issue matters, what information is missing and what to do next. The main user is an SME owner or person coordinating compliance work. An accountant, adviser or HR
colleague can use an exported report to discuss the business's situation with the owner. The current interface
does not include an invitation or team permission management screen; entering a responsible person's name
assigns a task label, not an account or access permission. The app uses three kinds of processing:
